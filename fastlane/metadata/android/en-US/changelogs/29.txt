NovaRedux's first release: Supernova reworked to match Nova Launcher 7.

- Nova's look: dock shapes and images, drawer card, circle reveals, night mode by part, folder animations
- Google Discover or a feed app as the page left of home, through a bridge app
- Web suggestions through the optional NovaRedux Connect app; NovaRedux stays offline
- Category tabs, two-finger gestures over widgets, adaptive icon animations
- Imports Nova backups, settings included
